# CareSetu Design System & Architecture Specification

## Tokens
- **Font Family:** 'Plus Jakarta Sans', system-ui, sans-serif
- **Primary Blue:** `#2563EB` (Tailwind `blue-600`)
- **Primary Hover:** `#1D4ED8` (Tailwind `blue-700`)
- **Teal / Cyan:** `#0D9488` (Tailwind `teal-600`) / `#14BBA6`
- **Soft Green:** `#10B981` (Tailwind `emerald-500`)
- **Deep Navy Text:** `#0F172A` (Tailwind `slate-900`)
- **Muted Text:** `#64748B` (Tailwind `slate-500`)
- **Surface Translucent:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`
- **Glass Border:** `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`
- **Ambient Shadow:** `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.08), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8) inset`

## Page Visual System (Global Backgrounds)
1. **Landing & Public Home:** `Caring Consultation in a Modern Clinic` hero photography with floating glass service cards.
2. **Login & Auth Experience:** `CareSetu Healthcare Login Experience` consultation backdrop with floating glass card and Patient/Doctor switcher.
3. **Patient Dashboard:** `CareSetu Hospital Reception Glow` softened background with layered glass cards.
4. **Search Page:** Soft medical atmosphere with category filter tabs.
5. **Doctors & Detail:** Clean clinical consultation depth with credentials, rating, and booking modal.
6. **Hospitals & Detail:** Architectural healthcare imagery with department breakdown and affiliated doctors.
7. **Appointments & Queue:** High-polish timeline with token tracker, current status, and countdown.
8. **Assistance Network:** Emergency Dial 112 / SOS banner + Blood Bank inventory locator with filter by blood group.
9. **Medicines & Generics:** Pharmacy catalog with generic alternatives, pricing, salt composition, and safety disclaimer.
10. **Health Vault:** Encrypted/protected records concept, prescription history, lab reports, and document uploader.
11. **Doctor Portal (Dashboard, Queue, Patients, Appointments):** Clinical workstation cockpit with live token caller and patient record viewer.
